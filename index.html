About the Game

Arcade racing with a focus on control

We wanted to focus on what makes racing games fun - nailing those perfect turns and feeling in complete control of your car.
Instead of long tracks, the game emphasizes tight sections where every turn matters.
Players improve through practice and mastering timing.

Track Design

Every corner

was designed with one thing in mind - making you feel like a drift master

We spent ages getting the flow right - you'll hit these long straights where you can breathe, then suddenly you're thrown into these crazy tight sections.
Visual cues help players anticipate movement while keeping the gameplay fast.
